"Sophia," I said as we reached the church steps. "I'm glad we ran into each other today."

She turned to look at me, something shifting in her expression. "Me too."

"And I hope we can do this again. Explore, I mean. Spend time together."

For a moment, she didn't answer. Then she smiled, the same genuine expression that had lit up her face in the café.

"I'd like that," she said. "I'd like that very much."

Standing there on the steps of a centuries-old church, with Sophia smiling at me in the Bermuda sunshine, I felt something I hadn't experienced in years.

Hope.
